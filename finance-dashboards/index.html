The boring part nobody does

A spreadsheet template can open beautifully and still be wrong. The formulas run, the cells fill, the numbers look plausible - and one of them is quietly lying to you. That is the failure a buyer cannot see and only finds out about later.

So before these were finished, every formula in all three was recalculated and checked against arithmetic worked out by hand - 25 assertions, and a sweep confirming zero error cells. That pass caught three defects, and each one was a wrong number that looked like a right number:

  • A client table that used an array formula needing Ctrl-Shift-Enter in Excel. It returned the first client and silently dropped the rest - so a freelancer with five clients would have seen one, with no error anywhere.
  • Churn and net revenue retention that averaged in months with no data yet, reading 1.6% where the true figure was 3.25%, and 100% where it was 98.6%.
  • A divide-by-zero dressed up by IFERROR as a tidy "$0.00 average growth" - an error presented as a result.

None of these would have shown up in a screenshot. All three would have shown up in a review.
