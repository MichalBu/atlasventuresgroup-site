xlcheck

openpyxl writes formulas but never evaluates them, so a generated workbook can build cleanly and be arithmetically wrong. xlcheck recalculates it the way Excel would and asserts every figure against hand-computed arithmetic — plus a sweep for error cells you didn't know to look for.
