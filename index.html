How a product gets built here

Three gates. Nothing gets past one on enthusiasm.

01 — Find

A pain someone already pays for

If nobody is spending money or hours on the problem today, there's nothing to sell. We look for the workaround before we look for the product.

02 — Test

The cheapest honest test

A real, buyable thing in front of real people — priced, not promised. Interest that doesn't survive a price tag isn't interest.

03 — Keep or kill

Revenue, or it's gone

Products earn their next step by making more than they cost to run. The ones that don't get killed quickly, which is what keeps the rest good.

What kind of company is this?

A software venture studio. We find expensive, specific problems — documented in real practitioner communities — and build focused, self-serve products that fix them. Products launch in early access, stay honest about what they can do, and grow only when they earn it.

Do I need an account to buy anything?

No. DashKit and DevPack are one-time purchases with instant delivery — no account, no subscription, no upsell sequence.

Who processes payments?

Payments on product pages are processed by our merchant of record, named at checkout. We never see or store your card details.

Is the Bookkeeping Cleanup Auditor available yet?

Not yet. It's in development and the waitlist is open. Early waitlist members get their first audit free and get paid access before anyone else. Nothing is charged for joining.

Will the Auditor change my books?

No. It's diagnostic only. You send an export, it returns a dated report of what looks wrong. It has no write access to QuickBooks, Xero or anything else.

Do your spreadsheet templates work in Google Sheets?

Yes — and in Excel, LibreOffice and Numbers. They're formulas only, with no macros or scripts, and we verify the same file produces the same numbers in all four.

Can you build something for us?

We don't do client work. But if you have an expensive, specific problem you'd pay to make go away, tell us about it — that's exactly where our products come from.
